# Prospect Happy Hour — budget categories

Default structure for a happy hour (typically 30–80 attendees, F&B-led, lighter than a dinner).

- Venue & F&B (~70%)
  - Venue rental / F&B minimum (~85% of category)
  - Bar service (~15% of category)
- Branded materials (~10%)
  - Signage
  - Take-aways / branded coasters / etc.
- Photography (~5%)
  - Photographer
- Gifts (light, optional) (~5%)
  - Small giveaways
- Staffing (~3%)
  - Onsite event manager
- Misc / Contingency (~7%)
  - Contingency
