# Customer Dinner — budget categories

Default structure for a customer dinner (~25 attendees). Percentages are starting suggestions of total budget; sub-line-items within a category split evenly unless noted.

- Venue (~35%)
  - Location Rental (~90% of category)
  - AV Equipment
  - AV Staff
  - Wi-Fi
- F&B (~28%)
  - Catering (~75% of F&B)
  - Beverages (~25% of F&B)
- Décor & Print (~6%)
  - Signage
  - Floral / centerpieces
  - Menus / name cards
- Gifts & Swag (~10%)
  - Branded gifts
- Photography (~3%)
  - Photographer
- Transportation (~4%)
  - Rideshare credits
- Staffing (~4%)
  - Onsite event manager
- Misc / Contingency (~10%)
  - Contingency
