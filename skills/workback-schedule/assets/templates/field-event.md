# Field Event — workback template

Default lead time: ~60 days. Field events run the spectrum from a city-based half-day workshop to an evening reception with a speaker. This template assumes a workshop or breakfast/lunch with content.

- T-60 | Confirm event goals, persona, target accounts, content theme | event-marketing
- T-55 | Speaker / content owner confirmed (internal or external) | event-marketing
- T-50 | Venue shortlist circulated | event-marketing
- T-45 | Venue booked, AV / room layout confirmed (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-42 | Content outline drafted by speaker | event-marketing
- T-35 | Invite list finalized with sales partners | sales-ops
- T-30 | Invite copy + landing page drafted | event-marketing
- T-28 | Invites sent | sales-ops
- T-21 | Content deck v1 reviewed | event-marketing
- T-21 | First RSVP nudge | sales-ops
- T-14 | F&B and AV confirmed; signage / handouts ordered | event-marketing
- T-14 | Content deck v2 reviewed | event-marketing
- T-10 | Final headcount estimate, attendee list shared with sales | event-marketing
- T-7 | Speaker rehearsal | event-marketing
- T-7 | AE briefing — talking points + target accounts | sales-ops
- T-3 | Confirm dietary, print badges / handouts | event-marketing
- T-1 | Venue walkthrough, AV check, AE huddle | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you with content recap and slides | sales-ops
- T+3 | CRM logging + meeting requests sent | sales-ops
- T+10 | Pipeline impact snapshot to leadership | event-marketing
