# Customer Dinner — workback template

Default lead time: ~60 days. Designed for 16–40 attendees; adjust per Step 11 in SKILL.md for other tiers.

- T-60 | Confirm event goals, persona, and budget with stakeholders | event-marketing
- T-55 | Build invite list with sales partners (target 2x final headcount) | sales-ops
- T-50 | Venue shortlist (5–8 options) circulated to stakeholders | event-marketing
- T-45 | Venue selected and place on hold | event-marketing
- T-40 | Venue contract signed and deposit sent (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-35 | Invite copy + dietary form drafted, legal review if needed | event-marketing
- T-30 | Save-the-date sent | sales-ops
- T-25 | Formal invite with RSVP link sent | sales-ops
- T-21 | Menu locked with venue, AV requirements confirmed | event-marketing
- T-18 | First RSVP nudge to non-responders via AE | sales-ops
- T-14 | Final headcount target confirmed; second RSVP nudge | event-marketing
- T-10 | Seating chart drafted with sales partners | event-marketing
- T-10 | Talking points + briefing doc shared with attending team | event-marketing
- T-7 | Day-of run-of-show drafted (arrivals, seating, cadence) | event-marketing
- T-5 | Pre-dinner team brief (15 min) with attending reps | event-marketing
- T-3 | Confirm final headcount, dietary, name cards, gifts | event-marketing
- T-2 | Print name cards, briefing doc, agenda | event-marketing
- T-1 | Venue walkthrough + AV check + arrival logistics | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you email with personal note from exec sponsor | sales-ops
- T+2 | Share photos / recap with attendees and stakeholders | event-marketing
- T+5 | CRM logging of attendees + next-step meetings booked | sales-ops
- T+10 | Post-event ROI snapshot to leadership | event-marketing
