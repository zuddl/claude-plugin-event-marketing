# Ancillary Tradeshow Event — workback template

Default lead time: ~90 days. Ancillary events run alongside a major conference (RSA, Black Hat, Dreamforce, AWS re:Invent, KubeCon). Sponsor rules, hotel block conflicts, and competing parties are unique constraints.

- T-90 | Confirm goals, target accounts attending the tradeshow | event-marketing
- T-90 | Check tradeshow sponsor rules — what's allowed for non-sponsors? | event-marketing
- T-85 | Confirm date and time slot (avoid sponsor parties + keynote conflicts) | event-marketing
- T-80 | Venue shortlist within walking / short-rideshare of convention center | event-marketing
- T-75 | Venue booked; lock contract before tradeshow demand spikes (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-70 | Pull attendee list from tradeshow registration partners / sales intel | sales-ops
- T-60 | Invite copy drafted, calendar block reserved | event-marketing
- T-55 | First-wave invites sent (target known attendees first) | sales-ops
- T-45 | Second-wave invites sent (broader prospect list) | sales-ops
- T-35 | First RSVP nudge | sales-ops
- T-30 | F&B menu locked with venue | event-marketing
- T-30 | Signage / branded materials ordered (tradeshow theming) | event-marketing
- T-21 | Second RSVP nudge; calendar conflict outreach | sales-ops
- T-21 | Speaker / talking points confirmed (if content-led) | event-marketing
- T-14 | Final headcount estimate; share attendee list with AEs | event-marketing
- T-10 | Attendee briefing pack to AEs (target accounts per rep) | sales-ops
- T-7 | Final F&B count; reconfirm room block / venue access | event-marketing
- T-7 | Onsite logistics plan: badge pickup, rideshare, escorts | event-marketing
- T-3 | Confirm dietary; print badges / name cards | event-marketing
- T-2 | Tradeshow-day reminder text/email to confirmed attendees | sales-ops
- T-1 | Venue walkthrough; AE huddle at hotel | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you with photos and follow-up offer | sales-ops
- T+3 | CRM logging; tradeshow follow-up plan kicks in | sales-ops
- T+14 | Pipeline impact snapshot vs. tradeshow ROI baseline | event-marketing
