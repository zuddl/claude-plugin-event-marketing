# Hackathon — workback template

Default lead time: ~90 days. Customer or developer hackathon. Themes, prizes, judging panel, and tooling access are the long-lead items.

- T-90 | Confirm goals (community / pipeline / product-feedback), persona, theme | event-marketing
- T-85 | Confirm format (in-person / hybrid / remote), duration, location | event-marketing
- T-80 | Venue shortlist (in-person component) | event-marketing
- T-75 | Venue booked; verify power, wifi, breakout rooms (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-70 | Judging panel invited (mix internal + external) | event-marketing
- T-65 | Prize structure decided (cash / swag / mentorship / job opportunity) | event-marketing
- T-60 | Sponsor list (if applicable) + prize sponsors confirmed | event-marketing
- T-55 | Registration landing page live | event-marketing
- T-50 | Marketing push begins (social, community channels, partner posts) | event-marketing
- T-45 | API / tooling access plan finalized with engineering | event-marketing
- T-42 | Mentor list confirmed (internal engineers signed up for shifts) | event-marketing
- T-35 | Theme / challenge prompts drafted | event-marketing
- T-30 | F&B plan (breakfasts, lunches, late-night snacks, coffee) | event-marketing
- T-30 | Swag designed and ordered | event-marketing
- T-25 | Judging rubric drafted and shared with panel | event-marketing
- T-21 | Mid-cycle marketing push; registration count check | event-marketing
- T-21 | Mentor onboarding doc sent | event-marketing
- T-14 | Final registration count; cap if needed | event-marketing
- T-14 | API / tooling access keys provisioned for registrants | event-marketing
- T-10 | Onsite logistics: signage, check-in, tables, power strips | event-marketing
- T-7 | Mentor and judge briefing call | event-marketing
- T-5 | Final F&B count to caterer | event-marketing
- T-3 | Print materials, swag pickup, prize procurement | event-marketing
- T-1 | Venue setup + AV check | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution (kickoff, hacking, judging, awards) | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you to participants, mentors, judges; photo recap | event-marketing
- T+3 | Winner announcement post-amplification (blog, social) | event-marketing
- T+7 | Participant follow-up: hiring conversations, product feedback | sales-ops
- T+14 | Outcomes report (community growth, pipeline, product feedback) | event-marketing
