# Executive Retreat — budget categories

Default structure for a multi-day C-level retreat at a destination. Lodging, travel, and experiences are the big rocks.

- Resort / Lodging (~30%)
  - Room block
  - Resort fees
- F&B (~20%)
  - Welcome dinner
  - Daily breakfasts / lunches
  - Closing dinner
  - Beverages
- Travel (attendees) (~10%)
  - Flights / first-class upgrades
  - Travel concierge fees
- Activities & Experiences (~12%)
  - Off-site experience (golf / spa / excursion)
  - Evening activities
- Speakers / Facilitators (~5%)
  - Speaker honoraria
  - Facilitator fees
- Gifts & Welcome Kits (~8%)
  - Welcome kits
  - Departure gift
- Branded materials (~3%)
  - Agenda books
  - Signage
- Ground transport (~4%)
  - Airport transfers
  - On-site transport
- Photography & Video (~3%)
  - Photographer
  - Recap video editor
- Staffing (~3%)
  - Onsite event manager
  - DMC / local coordinator
- Misc / Contingency (~2%)
  - Contingency
