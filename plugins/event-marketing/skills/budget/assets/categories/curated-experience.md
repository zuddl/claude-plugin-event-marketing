# Curated VIP Experience — budget categories

Default structure for a bespoke high-touch experience (F1 hospitality, Sphere tour, Michelin chef's table, museum buyout, golf with a pro). Experience provider fees dominate.

- Experience provider fees (~45%)
  - Provider contract
  - Provider extras / upgrades
- F&B (anchor venues) (~18%)
  - Pre-experience cocktails / dinner
  - Post-experience reception
- Travel (~10%)
  - Flights (attendees)
  - Hotel block
- Ground transport (~6%)
  - Private cars / SUVs
  - On-site shuttle
- Gifts (~8%)
  - Welcome gift
  - Branded keepsake from the experience
- Branded materials (~3%)
  - Itinerary / agenda book
- Photography & Video (~4%)
  - Photographer
  - Highlight reel editor
- Staffing (~3%)
  - Onsite event manager
- Misc / Contingency (~3%)
  - Contingency
