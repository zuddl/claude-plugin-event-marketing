# Curated VIP Experience — workback template

Default lead time: ~75 days. Bespoke high-touch experience for small VIP group: F1 hospitality, Sphere tour, golf with a pro, Michelin chef's table, museum buyout, helicopter tour, etc. Experience vendor is the long lead.

- T-75 | Confirm goals, persona, exec sponsor, budget tier | event-marketing
- T-72 | Experience type + provider shortlist (delegate to experience-research if needed) | event-marketing
- T-70 | Provider selected; availability confirmed | event-marketing
- T-65 | Contract signed with experience provider, deposit sent (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-60 | Invite list locked with exec sponsor (typically 6–20 ppl) | sales-ops
- T-55 | Save-the-date with high-level teaser | sales-ops
- T-50 | Travel coordination begins (flights, hotel block if needed) | event-marketing
- T-45 | Formal invite with experience details + dietary / accessibility form | sales-ops
- T-40 | Anchor activities around the experience (pre-event drinks, post-event dinner) | event-marketing
- T-35 | First RSVP nudge with exec sponsor personal note | sales-ops
- T-30 | Anchor venues booked (dinner / cocktails) | event-marketing
- T-25 | Branded gifting designed and ordered | event-marketing
- T-21 | Travel logistics confirmed (ground transport, escorts) | event-marketing
- T-14 | Final attendee list; provider final headcount lock | event-marketing
- T-14 | Briefing pack drafted for attending internal team | sales-ops
- T-10 | Detailed itinerary sent to attendees | sales-ops
- T-7 | Onsite team brief; talking points per attendee | sales-ops
- T-7 | Confirm provider logistics (arrival window, dress code, special access) | event-marketing
- T-3 | Final reconfirmation outreach | sales-ops
- T-2 | Gifts shipped or pre-positioned with venue | event-marketing
- T-1 | Onsite walkthrough with provider | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you from exec sponsor (handwritten for top tier) | sales-ops
- T+3 | Photo / video recap to attendees | event-marketing
- T+7 | CRM logging + follow-up meetings booked | sales-ops
- T+14 | Pipeline + relationship snapshot to leadership | event-marketing
