# Prospect Happy Hour — workback template

Default lead time: ~45 days. Lighter than a dinner; venue is often a private bar area or restaurant patio.

- T-45 | Confirm goals, persona, target accounts with sales | event-marketing
- T-40 | Invite list built with AE/SDR partners (target 3x final headcount) | sales-ops
- T-35 | Venue shortlist (private bar / lounge / patio options) | event-marketing
- T-30 | Venue booked, F&B minimum confirmed (see budget) | event-marketing
- T-25 | Invite copy drafted, sent to design if branded | event-marketing
- T-21 | Invites sent (email + LinkedIn for warm prospects) | sales-ops
- T-14 | First RSVP nudge | sales-ops
- T-10 | F&B selections confirmed with venue | event-marketing
- T-7 | Final headcount estimate, signage / branded materials ordered | event-marketing
- T-7 | AE briefing — talking points + target accounts per rep | sales-ops
- T-3 | Second RSVP nudge, confirm dietary | sales-ops
- T-1 | Venue walkthrough, AE pre-event huddle | event-marketing
- T-0 | Day-of execution | event-marketing
- T+1 | Thank-you and "next step" email per rep | sales-ops
- T+3 | CRM logging + meeting requests sent | sales-ops
- T+7 | Pipeline impact snapshot | event-marketing
